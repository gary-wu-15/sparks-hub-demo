Data notes and prototype rules

Every customer example has its own prototype link and component ranking below. Examples use fictional IDs and are labelled Customer 1, 2, and so on. Matching order labels mean the recommendation order is the same, even if scores differ.

Below the fixed Sparks hero, ranked sections are sorted by numeric rank, not score. Repeated components keep their highest-ranked position. Partner rewards is shown once below the ranked sections. Marketing banners follows its numeric rank when separately mapped and recommended; otherwise it appears once below Partner rewards. The content and balances are illustrative and identical initially; this test personalises component order, not individual offers.

Stamp cards are excluded on desktop, including completed rewards. On Tablet and App, an unranked stamp card appears once above Partner rewards. Rank-one coffee cards and Coffee Completion follow the top-of-hub reward rule, after Sparks offers when Sparks Tuesdays is enabled.

Wallets ranked 1 or 2 stay in the Sparks hero. Lower-ranked wallets appear as separate Your wallet sections at their individual ranks. Sparks rewards requires sparks_wallet. When credit_card_wallet is absent, its wallet appears immediately after Credit Card offers and the points-reward bar, regardless of the offers rank. Wallets are never duplicated. Sparks card access and How it works remain in the hero. Original recommendation ranks are shown below, not the hero's visual position.

sparks_card uses the existing Sparks card CTA and flyout; how_it_works uses rewards guidance; credit_card_offers uses the separate Credit Card rewards section; partnership_offers uses Partner rewards. baby_club uses “The parent hood” section from the master design. New missions and stamp interactions are illustrative, not live eligibility or rewards.

This public gallery uses fictional IDs, balances and illustrative rankings. Original customer IDs, scores and source files are not published. Edits and activity stay in your own browser. Do not enter real customer information.
